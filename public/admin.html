🎓 End-of-Year Promotion (advances every learner one grade, zone-wide)

This moves every learner up one grade across all schools: Grade 8→9, 7→8 … 1→2. Grade 9 leavers become Graduates (kept 1 year). Grade 1 is cleared for the new intake. Names and assessment numbers carry forward; marks reset for the new class. An automatic backup is taken per school first. This cannot be undone from here.
